Mr ANDENET TESFAYE Head of the Bureau As he said,the projects under Bureau, in addition to supporting the government's development efforts, have continued to strengthen their activities to ensure the benefit of the rural community in need. Subsequently,all programs presented their 2018 half-year plan performance reports and discussed them.
