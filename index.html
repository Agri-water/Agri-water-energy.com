MISSION VISSION AND VALUE OF OUR BEREAU

VISSION

Ensuring that the rural community within the Administration has reliable access to water resources through modern and participatory management practices. truEnabling the Dire Dawa Administration to become self-sufficient in the provision of sustainable, clean drinking water, irrigation coverage, mineral resources, and the development of effective alternative energy sources.

MISSION

The objective is to provide efficient and sustainable services to the community under its jurisdiction, while fostering social and economic development through the implementation of government-issued laws, regulations, policies, and strategies concerning the development, utilization, and management of agriculture, water, mineral, and energy resources.
