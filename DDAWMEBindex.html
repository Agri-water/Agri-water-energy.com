Mr Mahadi Mohamad The Head of Agri, Water Mine and Energy Beruea

M.r Mahadi Mahemad Head of the Bureau As he said,the projects under Bureau, in addition to supporting the government's development efforts, have continued to strengthen their activities to ensure the benefit of the rural community in need. In particular, the head of the bureau added that it is necessary to implement the activities planned for the first six months of the 2018 fiscal year and to put forward solutions to the challenges that exist. Subsequently,all programs presented their 2018 half-year plan performance reports and discussed them.
